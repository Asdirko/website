Info, About Me, FAQ

Wow, you made it past the front page! Apparently not many people do. I guess I should introduce myself.

I'm Ari, creator of everything on this site unless otherwise specified, or where something looks too good to be made by my clumsy fingers. I already have a blurb on the home page about why I made this site, so I'll just cut to the chase.

I'm (probably?) like your average next door neighbour who enjoys creative noodling when not busy doing life things. I like cream soda, dislike scrambled eggs and am very proud of the fact that I have kept several potted plants alive for over a year.

My favorite movies include Dune (the books are stellar too), Blade Runner, the Sergio Leone westerns, and Nausicaa. My candidate for favorite novel is a pretty close match between The Man Who Was Thursday and The Westing Game. Both are excellent and I'm not sure I'll ever decide. My music taste is far more erratic and less-defined than my taste in literature, but Boards Of Canada is one band that I can't help but come back to time and time again. Japanese Breakfast is awesome too. I also like origami, strong coffee, the works of Moebius, and indie video games.

Anyways, If need help looking for something or have questions, I recommend checking the site map or FAQ at your left. I (hopefully?) have organized everything in some sort of orderly fashion, but even the best librarians put books in the wrong section from time to time.
