Welcome Traveler! I'm Ari and this is my website full of whatever wierd junk I think of and/or make. I enjoy being creative as whenever I can, if only for the sake of making cool things. I'll do my best to update this site as often as I can, but life happens and things will often get in the way. I doubt anything will ever come of this place, but oh well. You have to enjoy the creative process for what it is, not for what you want it to be.

All that being said, have a look around if you'd like. This site is host to various personal projects of mine, ranging from blog posts and essay to music selections and art. This site itself is a pet project because I had the terrible idea that learning html would be fun (just kidding, it actually is a lot more than you'd think.)

My inspiration for this site stemmed from how much I dislike social media as an expressive medium (original opinion, I know.) I hate the corporate soulessness, the strict formats, and the constant focus on followers, likes, and views. As if simply talking to the world and engaging in meaningful conversation was taboo. It's a big deal to share a moment in life with say, two dozen people, but thats nothing in viewers if you want to be successful on twitch. It's the equivalent selling your soul to make a quick buck, and every day I'm impressed with how low some people will stoop to do so.

So in order to create something that felt more personal I just decided to make a website of my own. The less restrictive format is ideal for arranging thoughts and ideas in a meaningful way and truly making it a reflection of yourself. Personal websites are like some wierd mix of an art museum, coffee house or someones childhood treefort. A place where you're free to admire the scenery and exchange ideas or just hang out. Everybody puts their own spin on it and it becomes something new. As you can probably tell by now, this is about as far from corporate mega-mall platforms as you can get. In today's day and age where the dead internet theory is becoming a reality, partaking in the slower-paced, backwater routes of the internet comes with many small pleasures and gratifications. It's refreshing to see things made from genuine passion and interest rather than algorithmic engagement.

Well, looks like I started getting all deep and philosophical again. Rambling on in circles. If you don't mind listening to rambles, stick around and feel free to stroll through my webpages. Take in the view. Stop and smell the pine trees. Or the roses, I don't judge.
