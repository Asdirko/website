Media Recommendations

Everybody under the sun seems to have a list of things they like, whether that be movies, tv shows, novels, and so on. The things we relate to and associate with can often form who we are and want to be, influencing our actions and behaviour. Not everything has to be like this, and there is also nothing wrong with enjoying media for entertainment's sake and simply thinking it's fun. In fact, I see a lot of people look down on certain works just because it's not some profound meditation on the human condition or something. I've learnt to ignore such people who deride such simple joy, but I find it amusing that they think such a way. As if it's not possible to have both amusement and constructive, critical thought.

This list is a mix of both.
