    Art, Thoughts, Cool Things

    Greetings, and welcome to my internet hideout.

    This is a personal site full of my random projects, thoughts, etc. It's primarily a catalogue arranged for my own benefit, so bear that in mind if you come across a page with no apparent use.

    I was in a bit of a creative rut at the time I first created this site, and learning to code some basic html was a fun detour from my usual hobbies. That enterprise turned out much better than I expected, and this website right here is the result.

    It's basically just a place where I stretch my creative muscles and see if I'm capable of creating code that doesn't crash and burn. Feel free to look around.

    This site does get worked on and edited over time, so if parts of it are unfinished, poorly formatted or incomplete, I apologize. Just like myself, this site is a perpetual work in progress.
